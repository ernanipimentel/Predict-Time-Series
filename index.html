About Me

A Passion for Languages. A Gift for Teaching.

I am a native French speaker and qualified Advanced Skills Teacher with over 15 years' experience teaching in London secondary schools. I have also trained fellow teachers to teach French, Spanish and German at all levels — and I speak 5 languages fluently.

As a former examiner for one of England's main Exam Boards, I bring unique insight into exactly what assessors look for. My students consistently make rapid, measurable progress — often within the very first lesson.

I follow a revolutionary teaching method that demystifies languages and makes them genuinely accessible. GCSE students regularly achieve Grade 9 writing after just one year. If you are not satisfied with your progress in the first lesson, I will refund the cost in full.

Each learner is different, and I adapt every session to the individual — something a classroom simply cannot offer. Seeing my students gain confidence is the greatest reward.
