FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Lessons take place via Zoom or Google Meet — whichever you prefer. You'll receive a link before each session. I use an interactive whiteboard, screen sharing, and video so sessions feel just as engaging as face-to-face. All you need is a stable internet connection, a device with a camera and microphone, and a notepad.

I teach all levels from complete beginner through to university entry. For school students this covers Key Stage 3 (Years 7–9), GCSE and IGCSE (Years 10–11), and A-Level / IB (Years 12–13). For adults, I work with all CEFR levels from A1 to C2, including business language and conversational practice.

Absolutely — and as a multilingual tutor I can help you leverage the similarities between languages (especially within the Romance family: French, Spanish, Portuguese). Many of my students study two languages simultaneously. I'll design separate lesson plans for each and can even highlight cross-language patterns to accelerate your progress in both.

I have extensive experience with AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC (Eduqas), and Cambridge IGCSE across all four languages. For IB students I cover both Standard Level and Higher Level. I'll align every lesson to the specific mark scheme and assessment objectives of your exam board.

Payment is made by bank transfer or PayPal before each lesson or at the start of a monthly pack. I ask for 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a lesson — late cancellations (under 24 hours) are charged at 50%. There are no long-term contracts; you can stop or pause lessons at any time.

Yes — all materials are included in the lesson price. After each session I send a PDF summary of what we covered, supplementary exercises, and audio resources. For exam students I provide past papers, model answers, and vocabulary lists tailored to your exam board. There is no need to purchase any textbook.
